How it works

A merchant creates an invoice. The buyer pays tUSD8 into escrow. Funds release when the buyer confirms delivery — or to the merchant after 7 days with no confirmation. The merchant can refund any time before release; the buyer can open a dispute. Pay with a normal approval, a one-transaction EIP-2612 permit, or gaslessly: an agent can submit your EIP-3009 signature (scripts/agent-pay.mjs).
